AGREE BEFORE ELECTION DAY

Independent Representation. Fuel Security First.

Show Voters The Agreement Before Asking For Their Votes.

New Zealanders deserve to know what their vote could deliver before election day.

I propose that Independent candidates and willing smaller parties publish a Confidence And Supply Agreement before the election—giving voters a clear choice about representation, leadership and priorities.

The Opportunity

Our campaign’s working count is 48 Independent candidates across 34 electorates. The next step is to invite compatible smaller parties to discuss cooperation in further electorates.

The aim is to build enough support in Parliament for an Independent-led Government, with Fuel Security as its first shared priority.

Candidate numbers are a starting point. Every seat must still be won.

What Should They Agree On?

A short, public agreement should explain:

  • Who they would support to lead a Government.
  • How they would vote on confidence and supply, including government funding.
  • What action they would take on fuel security, including an assessment of restoring domestic refining capacity.
  • Which decisions would remain open to conscience votes.
  • How electorate MPs would report back to their communities.
  • How disagreements would be resolved.

Independent representation must mean accountability to the electorate. Cooperation must be clear enough for voters to judge.

Two Votes. A Clear Choice.

The proposed campaign message is:

ELECTORATE VOTE: Back Your Independent Or An Explicitly Endorsed Partner Candidate.

PARTY VOTE: Support A Participating Party Whose Policies And Published Agreement You Support.

This would change One Tick Independent’s earlier “no party vote” position. That change should follow a signed, published agreement so voters can see exactly what they are being asked to support.

Candidates standing for participating parties would remain party candidates. Their commitment to supporting an Independent-led Government would be recorded openly.

Give People A Reason To Participate

Many New Zealanders feel unheard. A practical agreement can give them something concrete to consider: local representation, a shared fuel-security programme and a clear basis for stable government.

No campaign can claim the votes of people who stay home. We must earn their participation.

What Needs To Happen Now?

Bring willing candidates and parties together. Agree on the essentials. Publish the terms, the signatories and the endorsed candidates.

Then put the proposition to the public:

Local Accountability. A Public Agreement. Fuel Security First.

Agree Before Election Day. Let Voters Decide.
